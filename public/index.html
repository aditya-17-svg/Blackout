Q&A

Questions, answered.

One person hosts and shares a six-letter code or invite link. Everyone joins, the host picks the seekers, and the rest become runners. Runners get a head start to hide, then the seekers hunt while the circle shrinks and pushes everyone together.

No. The game runs in your phone’s browser on iPhone, Android and desktop. Just open the link, sign in and allow location when asked.

Runners can see the seekers on their map at all times. Seekers only see where runners are at each ping, which the host sets, for example every 5 minutes. In between, they have to work it out.

When a seeker reaches a runner, they tap Tag and choose that runner’s name. The runner gets a prompt on their own phone and confirms or disputes it, so nobody can be tagged by mistake.

You get a warning and a countdown. If you stay outside the circle for two minutes, you are counted as tagged. The circle shrinks during the round, so keep an eye on it.

Your position is only used during a round. Runners’ locations stay hidden from seekers except at pings, and tagged players stop sharing completely. Nothing is shared when you are not in a game.

Yes. You get a live timer, distance, pace and estimated calories, with Run, Walk and Ride modes. Oxygen and altitude need a compatible device, and smartwatch sync is on the way.

Players must be 13 or older, and under-18s should have a parent’s permission. Stay off roads, keep out of private property, play in daylight with friends, and never chase anyone into danger. The game is not worth an accident.
