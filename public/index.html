No. Blackout plays in your browser on iPhone, Android and desktop. Open the link, sign in and allow location when asked.

One person hosts and shares a six-letter code or invite link. Everyone joins, the host picks the seekers, and the rest become hiders. Hiders get a head start, then the seekers hunt while the circle shrinks and pushes everyone together.

Hiders see seekers continuously. Seekers only receive intermittent hider pings, so a last-known location is a clue, not a guarantee.

When a seeker reaches a hider, they tap Tag and choose that hider’s name. The hider confirms or disputes it on their own phone, so nobody is tagged by mistake.

The zone shrinks as the game progresses. Watch the boundary and plan your next move before your hiding spot is outside the circle. Stay outside for two minutes and you are counted as tagged.

Your position is only used during a round. Hiders’ locations stay hidden from seekers except at pings, and tagged players stop sharing completely. Nothing is shared when you are not in a game.
